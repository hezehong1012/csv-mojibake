FAQ

Q. Why does my CSV file turn into garbled text?
A. Mojibake happens when the file's encoding does not match the encoding the app uses to read it.
Q. Which encoding should I choose?
A. Use UTF-8 with current Excel versions and Google Sheets. For older Excel setups, Shift-JIS is usually the safer choice.
Q. Where are my files and text stored?
A. This site runs entirely in your browser and never uploads your data to a server.
